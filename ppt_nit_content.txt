COURSE ALLOCATION
Fairness-Weighted Elective Allocation
A structured approach to distributing limited elective seats — balancing student preferences with historical allocation equity. This walkthrough demonstrates how the Fairness-Weighted Elective Allocation Algorithm (FWEAA) works in practice.
